## Is the new generation finally taking center stage?

One of the most frequently discussed topics regarding the upcoming EA Sports FC titles is the issue of older consoles. The PlayStation 4 and Xbox One have long been holding back the series’ development, and players are increasingly calling for a full focus on the new platforms.

However, as far as available platforms are concerned, Electronic Arts has not decided to make any changes. EA Sports FC 27 will be released on PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2, as well as on PC (via the EA app, Steam, and the Epic Games Store).

The game will also offer the “Dual Entitlement” program. Thanks to this program, owners of a PS4 or Xbox One version will be able to upgrade to the corresponding next-gen console edition at no additional cost.

## Gameplay: Greater Realism and Smoother Movement

In terms of gameplay, don’t expect a sudden revolution, but rather a refinement of what we already know. EA has been developing its animation and artificial intelligence systems for several years, and EA Sports FC 27 will mark another step toward more realistic soccer.

This primarily involves improved player behavior off the ball, more natural physical duels, and a greater impact of tactical choices on the flow of the game. High hopes are also pinned on the continued development of HyperMotion technology.

One of the most significant new features in EA Sports FC 27 is the “True-to-Football Gameplay” system, which aims to make gameplay more realistic and responsive. The developers have focused on improving player control and refining animations, making players’ movements smoother and more natural. Corner kicks have also been redesigned, player AI has been improved, and numerous fixes have been implemented in response to feedback from the gaming community.

The game will also introduce “Attacking Spatial Awareness,” a system designed to manage players’ movements off the ball. Players will interpret their teammates’ intentions more effectively, make better use of open spaces, and position themselves more intelligently to receive passes. The second new feature is “Off-Balance Dribbling,” which allows players to maintain control of the ball even after physical contact with an opponent. As a result, dribbling should be more dynamic and offer more possibilities for experienced players.

## The Grounds is the main new feature in EA Sports FC 27

The main new feature in EA Sports FC 27, however, is “The Grounds.” This will be a brand-new open community space inspired by street soccer. Players will be able to move freely within this space, develop their characters, participate in 1-on-1 duels and team matches, and take on various challenges. This is the biggest change to the series’ formula since the FIFA brand was replaced by EA Sports FC.

## Career Mode: A Last Chance to Turn the Tide?

The Manager Career mode has also undergone changes. The developers have revamped the transfer market, allowing player valuations to better reflect the realities of modern soccer. Negotiation options have also been expanded: now, when signing contracts, it’s possible to include, among other things, buyback clauses or bonuses tied to a player’s performance. Player morale and physical condition also play a more significant role. Current form affects not only on-field performance but also market value, making club management even more demanding in terms of decision-making.

When it comes to licenses, EA Sports FC 27 holds no surprises. The biggest leagues, clubs, and competitions are all still featured, and the publisher will continue to expand its database of stadiums, facial scans, and visual details. After several years of developing its own brand, EA Sports FC is demonstrating more and more clearly that the absence of the FIFA logo does not necessarily mean a lack of content. The game features more than 21,000 licensed soccer players, as well as 800 clubs and national teams. Added to that are, as of now, 35 leagues and 140 stadiums: so there’s certainly no shortage of content.
